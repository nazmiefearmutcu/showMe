## Global constraints
- Preserve unrelated MEET edits and don't stage them.
- No added runtime dependency, purchased source, fake total or synthetic fallback.
- One VCAP command; no hard-coded paid report dataset.
- Source dates, measurement periods, import date and release versions remain distinct.
- Local imports and credentials stay local; no arbitrary fetch URLs or paths.

## Review focus
1. Unit multipliers and GDP % must not silently corrupt values.
2. Missing periods/amounts and partial/revised releases must remain distinguishable from zero/final.
3. Multi-source/country/crypto overlap must not produce invented global totals or misleading comparisons.
4. Imports/revisions must be atomic, bounded, reproducible and not corrupt an existing store.
5. A real rendered chart, palette integration, responsive layout, and usable import/export must be verified.
